Stage-matched root extensions · v2

Press Extend root to add a painted fork. The original plant and root image stays unchanged.

Representative Cherry specimens: leafy Sprout, Sapling and Young Tree. Same fixed framing before and after each action.

Isolated compositor prototype, not the live game. Two generated families—ivory juvenile roots and reddish-brown woody roots—are attached to opaque pixels in roots rendered by the current game. Four additions per specimen demonstrate joins and density; these are illustrative, not gameplay thresholds. The fade demonstrates placement, not the final tip-growth animation. Each stage starts with its own existing root painting; cross-stage continuity still needs calibration and animation testing.
