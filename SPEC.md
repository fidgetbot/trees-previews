# Trees previews

## Playable painted-root integration

`playable-root-joins/` is a separate full-game build exported from `fidgetbot/trees` branch `painted-join-integration`, source commit `5467a49`. It integrates the 24 approved patches into the actual game renderer after Cherry-first lifecycle and visual review, then applies the same treatment to Plum, Apricot, Peach, Pear and Citrus. Seed through Seedling retain existing artwork and mechanics. The ordinary Seed opening remains playable; `?review=1` adds clearly labeled specimen controls, resource refill and milestone-assisted real stage advancement.

The runtime removes the study's brown matte, omits original painted-root fragments below the collar, and uses the source collar for trunk, foliage and root placement. Root IDs and canonical paths persist across woody stages; the network scales with the growing trunk, rather than remaining fixed in world coordinates. Root/depth counters remain authoritative and JSON-compatible; this does not add a save UI. Each asset is an exact copy of its approved patch, with hashes and source provenance beside the runtime images. A bounded root-layer cache prevents repeated camera measurements from consuming the growth-animation duration. Production `/trees/` is untouched. The separate gallery remains available as the artwork reference.

Separate static review site, independent of the Trees game repository and deployment.

## Painted roots

`painted-roots/index.html` is a self-contained browser prototype with embedded PNG layers from actual Cherry Sprout, Sapling and Young Tree game-rendered specimens and one generated feeder-root extension sprite. Extend Root adds up to four overlays; Reset removes them. Each specimen maintains fixed framing during additions. No game saves, gameplay rules, external services or storage are used.

Revision 2 replaces the universal golden root with two generated sprite families: soft ivory for Sprout and reddish-brown woody forks for Sapling and Young Tree. Display length varies by stage; attachment width is capped at 80% of measured parent-root width. Source PNGs are retained under `painted-roots/assets/`.

This is a compositor study, not a complete root-growth implementation. The fade is not final tip-growth animation. Between-stage continuity remains unvalidated. The three stage cards are independent examples, not sequential transitions. Mature and Ancient stage-specific families are not part of this three-stage study.

Hosted through GitHub Pages from main, repository root. No dependency on or mutation of the live game's version or files.

## Root-tip branching

Revision 7 preserves the entire painted base root system. The interactive central-taproot study starts with two slots and no added laterals. Grow roots fills a free slot or, when full, permanently adds a thinner branch to the least-developed existing lateral. Grow taproot extends downward and adds two slots; existing lateral anchors and investments remain unchanged. Reset restores the baseline. Fixed framing accommodates all three demo taproot extensions; 24 root investments is a review-only limit. New wood remains procedural pending art approval; production is unchanged.

Revision 6 adds an isolated central-taproot anatomy study. A tapered central axis carries stable alternating lateral attachment slots; laterals and their secondary roots are thinner than their parents. The old painted distal roots fade out below the retained collar in this mode. Existing tip-attachment comparisons remain available. This is a geometry study using procedural wood, not final painted bark or a production gameplay integration.

`painted-roots-short-stem/` compares the shipped connector, the earlier 65%-shorter study, and a tip-attached concept across Sapling, Young Tree, Mature Tree, and Ancient Cherry paintings. The tip-attached concept removes the narrow extension stem entirely and overlaps the extension's first fork slightly beneath the detected painted-root endpoint. The stage painting masks the seam, while the fork/fine-root artwork, scale, angles, counts, and framing remain unchanged. This is a prototype only; the production game is unchanged.

Revision 3 replaces connector-length comparisons with a layered-growth study. The previous tip fan remains available only as a baseline. The two new variants add deterministic tapered strands from beneath the root flare, distribute fine laterals along each strand, and place successive growth layers into gaps across the existing root spread. The denser option adds one strand and one lateral per growth layer. Both preserve the production stage painting and fixed framing; neither changes production assets or mechanics.

## Root-free base / unified procedural roots

`root-free-growth/` is an isolated Cherry study covering Sapling, Young, Mature and Ancient. A source-rectangle compositor keeps the original above-ground painting and omits all painted roots below a measured trunk cut; sources are unmodified. The attachment centre, width and colour are measured from the opaque trunk at that cut, not the image centre. All starting roots and additions use one tapered procedural wood renderer wrapping sampled trunk bark around gently irregular curves. Stage profiles begin with 5/7/9/11 structural roots. Stable seeded curves remain unchanged when finer laterals and rootlets fill interior gaps. Independent presets (0/8/20 investments) and close-up snapshots explain development; Grow branching roots adds one investment, Grow deeper roots adds connected deep laterals and a curved distal continuation, and Reset restores the starting state. Fixed framing accommodates demo limits of 24 branching and three deep investments. Roots are not capacity-gated by taproot length. The four age profiles are independent examples; age-transition continuity, final painted texture and production integration are not claimed.


## Botanical branching growth study

`botanical-root-growth/` is a separate follow-up to the root-free study. Five persistent main axes start short and slender. A deterministic parent/child graph reveals tip extension and three orders of lateral branching from alternating flanks. Attachment positions and existing paths never move. Older axes thicken gradually; child widths are capped at 58% of the parent's local width and taper to fine tips. Each of 24 growth steps advances this same network, rather than adding parallel main roots. Controls include animated Advance growth, Play/Pause, a scrubber, presets, Reset and close-up inspection. Reduced-motion settings make individual steps immediate. Fixed framing and the unchanged above-ground source painting are retained for all four independent Cherry age examples. Root snapshots stack at phone widths. This is a simplified illustrative model, not a species-accurate simulation or an age-transition implementation. Production and the previous prototypes are unchanged.


Botanical revision 2 staggers the four age stages along one shared lifetime root graph (six model-time units / 24 UI steps per stage). Established is now the actual stage endpoint (24), exactly equal to the following stage's starting geometry. Shared scaffold coordinates, origin spacing, root material and camera scale prevent size regression or root rearrangement at handoff. The original above-ground painting and its short collar change with stage; root identity, width and progress do not. Next tree stage is enabled at the stage endpoint; tabs select stage beginnings. Paired boundary previews show each handoff at equal scale. This supersedes revision 1's independent stage examples; production is unchanged.

Botanical revision 3 is an art-direction study for a less diagrammatic root system. It keeps revision 2's persistent lifetime and exact handoffs, but replaces the radial fan with five unequal, asymmetrically placed structural axes. The deepest axis bends off centre; branch timing, spacing, angle and length vary deterministically; widths and origins derive from the measured collar. A single continuous bark-textured buttress crown replaces separate vertical connector strips. Continuous highlights and sparse cross-grain marks replace short repeated bark slices, while a light soil glaze reduces the appearance of roots floating over the ground. Production remains unchanged pending visual approval.

Botanical revision 4 removes the synthetic buttress crown entirely. The compositor reveals a short additional band from the unmodified original painting so its real bark and painted root flare overlap the generated roots. Cherry remains the interactive lifetime study, while a paired Mature Cherry/Plum preview demonstrates that the same join treatment works across contrasting species. Production remains unchanged pending visual approval.

## Localized Cherry join study

`cherry-root-join/` isolates a single Mature Cherry attachment experiment, authorized by Nicolas on 2026-10-23. A fixed five-axis scaffold and the original trunk form the edit reference; one built-in image-generation edit supplies a locally repainted flare matched to those outgoing root paths. A feathered elliptical donor patch is composited over the live roots, not used as a replacement whole-tree image. The original canopy, unedited upper trunk and distal procedural network remain unchanged outside the mask. Reference, generated donor, exact original Cherry master, final patch and provenance are retained in `assets/`. Before/after close-ups, full-tree view and a growth scrubber make the actual composite reviewable. Primary geometry and texture sampling are stable during tip extension. This is a bounded one-stage visual proof: species rollout, stage continuity and final distal-root art are not implemented or claimed. Production and botanical v4 are untouched. Chromium/WebKit phone and desktop checks cover actual viewport size, asset loading, visible growth and zero pixel differences outside the donor mask.

## Species/stage painted join atlas

`painted-joins/` expands the approved localized artwork technique to six species (Cherry, Plum, Apricot, Peach, Pear, Citrus) × four woody stages (Sapling, Young, Mature, Ancient). Each specimen has its own unmodified source painting, collar measurement, rendered attachment reference and localized donor patch. Mature Cherry reuses the approved original patch. A manifest records candidate versus approved status; the gallery provides close-up, full-tree, before/after and animated growth review. Source images, exact generation prompts, raw donors, normalized alpha-masked patches, attachment contracts and hashes are retained. Bulk generation uses the explicitly approved non-posting OpenClaw image editor.

The 24 cards are independent visual specimens, not sequential states of one tree: their root scaffold scales to each measured source collar. Cross-stage root identity, save migration and production integration remain separate work. Juvenile artwork and the production game are unchanged. Distal root geometry retains the earlier study's known angular appearance; this atlas addresses the painted trunk/root join.
